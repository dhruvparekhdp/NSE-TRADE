Rules that never bend (AGENTS.md)

  • Real orders need mode=live and live_orders=true, set by the owner in the server .env. Code and tests never set them.
  • No secrets in code, tests, logs, commits or chat. Keys live encrypted in Postgres (/keys) or the server .env.
  • Strategies, costs, risk and exits are pure functions shared by the lab and the engine. Paper must equal the backtest on the same bars.
  • Research only through lab/evaluate.py, counted in the ledger, pre-registered in docs/PREREG.md. A change after seeing results is a new, post-hoc trial.
  • UTC epoch ms stored, IST shown. Prices on the tick. LIMIT / STOPLOSS_LIMIT only (Angel bans market and IOC for algos). 5 orders per second.
  • No deploy Mon–Fri 09:00–15:45 IST. Under 600 MB RAM on the server.

2 · Summary

Today (23 Oct 2026)

  • No paper trading runs: every trading job in the runner is a stub that only logs.
  • All research data is synthetic (source = "synth"). The S6 report is a smoke test.
  • Backtests are wrong in ways that flatter results: shorts never stopped (−30R in a probe), gap-down stops filled at the stop (−1.5R recorded vs about −10.6R real), mirror/random baselines invented.
  • The paper broker never adds intraday profit or loss to cash.
  • Four of the six intraday strategies can never fire: nothing supplies their inputs.
  • Market rules moved: the closing auction (since 3 Aug 2026) ends F&O stocks' continuous trading at 15:15, and the 15:05 square-off may now be after Angel's. Muhurat 2026 is in the afternoon, not the evening.
  • The portal has the crypto look but placeholder pages. Keys are typed over plain HTTP.
  • Nothing is committed yet. Angel login fails (AB1050, TOTP/client mismatch).

Target

  • The crypto engine's concept on NSE: one book paper trades (Book B daily swing, CNC), intraday (Book A) runs shadow only, every signal logged with mirror + random twins and replayed to an outcome, filters audited as taken vs skipped.
  • A ₹ paper cycle wallet with costs split the Indian way: STT, stamp, exchange, SEBI, GST, DP, brokerage.
  • A market layer that knows sessions by date and stock class, holidays, ticks, bands, T+1, surveillance and corporate actions.
  • An opening and sentiment recorder: GIFT Nifty, US, Europe, Asia, FX, crude, FII/DII, India VIX, tagged war and macro news, the pre-open auction and the first 30 minutes. Information first, a filter only if the lab proves it.
  • The crypto "Brutal Command" portal, clubbed to 7 pages.
  • Go-live only through gates, and only with the owner's written yes.

4 · Review: what works, what's broken

3.1 What works

  • Settings, the dual live flags and the pytest guard. Live order methods are hard-gated (broker/angel.py:107-110).
  • Cost model formulas (core/costs.py) match Angel's structure. Only the rates need refreshing (V1-4).
  • Indicators (SMA, EMA, Wilder ATR and RSI, VWAP, Donchian, Keltner), SQLite migrations, ledger, keys page with an encrypted store, Telegram client, market-hours deploy guard.
  • Tests: 54 pass. Ruff has 2 errors (unused imports in scripts/_remote_check_keys_quotes.py:6 and scripts/login_check.py:19), so CI is red.

3.2 What isn't there yet

  • No paper trading runs. Every trading job in engine/runner.py:34-59 only logs. /api/today returns zeros (web/portal_api.py:29-33). /api/health hard-codes login_ok: false (web/app.py:38).
  • All research data is synthetic. Every file in data/lake/bars_1d/ has source == "synth". The S6 report (45 trades, −0.26R) is a smoke test, not evidence.
  • The portal is a skin with placeholders. It has the crypto look and four hubs, but none of crypto's pages (swing positions in R, paper cycle tiles, signals with "why refused", taken vs skipped, go-live gates, session guard, accuracy, history), one palette instead of four, and no component kit.
  • Nothing is committed. The whole tree is untracked on feat/mvp-backtest-news (only "Initial commit" exists).

2. Decisions this plan proposes (owner confirms in §11)

  • D1. Book B paper trades first, on end-of-day data. Signals are computed on the daily close and filled the next day by exactly the backtest's daily fill rule. Paper doesn't depend on a live tick feed, and paper equals backtest by construction (crypto lesson 4).
  • D2. Book A runs shadow only (like crypto's 15m detectors). PLAN A2's own numbers mean Book A can't size a trade at ₹50k: the ₹1L minimum with 1% risk needs a stop of 0.5% or less, which costs 0.29R. It runs on the live Angel feed in V6, logs signals and virtual outcomes, and is promoted only through the gate.
  • D3. Paper capital ₹1,00,000 with 1% risk. That gives ₹20k-plus CNC positions at about a 5% stop. At ₹50k and 5 slots each position is ₹10k, and costs reach 0.79% a round trip (MARKET_INDIA.md §5). New setting min_position_cnc_inr = 20000.
  • D4. Square off ≥15 minutes before the broker's cut-off, derived from one setting, not hard-coded. CAS moved brokers' cut-offs to about 15:00–15:10 for F&O stocks until 6 Dec 2026.
  • D5. Lab first, then filters. The Nifty vol filter, a trend filter and one momentum strategy are added as pre-registered trials (V3). Nothing is switched on without passing the gate.
  • D6. One fill/exit engine (core/exits.py) for backtest, paper and outcome replay.
  • D7. The portal is a port of crypto's micro-UI (Store, widgets, views, components, palettes), with NSE data behind the same kind of JSON endpoints.
  • D8. Track the whole opening and the market's mood every day, from as early as possible (owner, 23 Oct 2026: "market opening is a very big thing, so track entire sentiment with GIFT Nifty, US and Europe market, war news, every major thing"). Record global cues, GIFT Nifty, FII/DII, India VIX, tagged major-event headlines, the 09:00–09:15 pre-open auction and the first 30 minutes. Pre-open and live breadth can't be downloaded later, so this is phase V1b, right after V1. It is information and research data: it never blocks or sizes a trade until a pre-registered lab trial shows lift (crypto: news echoed price 48/48).

Price bands. Non-F&O stocks have fixed daily bands (2/5/10/20%). F&O stocks have dynamic bands: an operating range (typically 10%) that the exchange widens step by step when price presses against it. 6. Rules for API (algo) trading, Angel SmartAPI, from 1 Apr 2026

  • Static IP: API orders are accepted only from the registered primary static IP. A secondary IP can be added. The mapped IP can be changed at most once a calendar week. Data and login calls are not tied to the IP.
  • Order types: market and IOC orders are prohibited for algo orders (some brokers convert market orders to market-price-protection orders, but Angel prohibits them). The engine uses LIMIT and STOPLOSS_LIMIT only, which PLAN already says.
  • Speed: a 10 orders-per-second threshold per exchange segment, counting new orders, modifications and cancels. Below it no strategy registration is needed and orders carry a generic algo ID. We throttle at 5/s.
  • Login: one session per trading day with 2FA (client code + MPIN + TOTP). No long-lived refresh tokens.
  • Hosting: retail algos run on the broker's servers, except "tech-savvy" clients who run their own logic from a registered static IP. That is us (EC2 Elastic IP).

7. Tax (for the CA export)

  • Delivery (Book B): short-term capital gains (held 12 months or less) are taxed at 20%. Long-term gains are taxed at 12.5% above ₹1.25 lakh a year. Budget 2026 didn't change these.
  • Intraday (Book A): speculative business income, taxed at the slab rate, filed in an ITR-3 style return.
  • The Income Tax Act 2025 replaces the 1961 Act from tax year 2026-27, and section numbers changed. The tax export should label rows by category (intraday-speculative / STCG / LTCG / charges split), not by section number. The owner's CA maps them.

10. Base rates and strategy evidence

  • SEBI study (FY 2022-23): 71% of individual intraday traders in the cash segment lost money. Among those making more than 500 trades a year, it was 80%. Under-30s did worst (76%). Costs were a large part of it.
  • The owner's crypto engine: intraday direction was a coin flip (walk-forward AUC 0.52). The only lasting edge was multi-day breakouts (+0.35R a trade after fees over 5 years). AI judgement and news filters never improved it.
  • Momentum in India: academic work finds both time-series and cross-sectional momentum in Indian stocks after standard risk adjustments, with sharp crashes in crisis and recovery phases. NSE's Nifty200 Momentum 30 index (6- and 12-month volatility-adjusted momentum, rebalanced twice a year) has led other large-cap indices on rolling returns. It also had the deepest drawdowns, including about −30% from Sep 2024 to Mar 2025.
  • Post-earnings drift: mixed. Older, longer studies (2002–2011) find drift, especially after bad news. A smaller 2014–2018 study finds none. Treat it as a hypothesis to test, not an edge.
  • Conclusion for the design: keep the crypto lesson. Daily-bar swing or momentum on liquid large caps is where an edge is plausible and costs are smallest per unit of risk. Intraday stays a shadow experiment until it passes the gate.
