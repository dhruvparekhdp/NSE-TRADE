# Backtest round 1: Book B 2020-2026, BTST / STBT on news 2019-2026, delta hedging 2020-2026

Run by Claude on 25 Oct 2026 from Angel One data (read-only download): daily bars 2019-01 -> 2026-10-23 for the
Nifty 100, NIFTY 50, NIFTY BANK, INDIA VIX; 1-minute index bars 2020-01 -> 2026-10-23. Scripts are the scratch
diagnostics described in `docs/CURSOR_NEXT.md` C5 / `docs/PLAN_V9_OPTIONS.md` V9-3. **Everything here is
diagnostic**: not run through `lab/evaluate.py`, not counted in the ledger, many variants were looked at. Book A
(intraday, 1-min, per FY) is still running and will be added as round 2.

Caveats that apply to every number:
- Universe is TODAY's Nifty 100 (survivorship bias: past results look better than reality).
- Option prices are MODELLED (Black-Scholes, IV = India VIX, Bank Nifty IV = 1.25 x VIX), not real quotes. Real ATM
  IV usually rises into a scheduled event and drops after it; VIX misses part of that, so the model is too kind to
  BUYING options before events and too harsh on selling them.
- Option round trip cost 2% of premium; futures 0.015% of notional per side.

## 2. BTST / STBT ATM options on scheduled news (Nifty; Bank Nifty is similar)

Average return on premium per trade, entry at the 15:25 close before the event. "Ordinary" is the same trade on
every other night.

| Event (n) | Exit | Call | Put | Straddle | Ordinary call / put / straddle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| RBI policy (48) | next open | **+7.6%** | -12.8% | -1.9% | +0.1 / -9.9 / -4.6 |
| RBI policy (48) | next close | +2.1% | -8.9% | -3.1% | -4.4 / -5.9 / -5.2 |
| FOMC (46) | next open | +2.8% | -2.2% | +0.4% | +0.1 / -9.9 / -4.6 |
| Union Budget (10) | Budget-day close | -4.9% | **+22.0%** | **+4.5%** | -4.4 / -5.9 / -5.2 |
| Election result (2) | close | -77% | +215% | +59% | anecdotes, n=2 |
| Exit polls (2) | next open | +117% | -81% | +21% | anecdotes, n=2 |

- **RBI BTST call, exit at the next open:** positive in both halves (+10.6% in 2019-22, +4.4% in 2023-26).
  The RBI announces at 10:00, so this exit comes BEFORE the decision. It is a pre-policy morning drift, not a
  reaction to the news. Holding through the decision to the close gives back most of it.
- **RBI by decision (close exit, Nifty):** cut: call -18.8%, put +11.8% ("sell the news"); hold: call +13.0%,
  put -21.1%; hike: both lose. The decision isn't known at the 15:25 entry, so this only helps if you have a
  reliable read of the consensus.
- **FOMC:** nights move more than ordinary nights (0.56% vs 0.43% at the open), so a straddle roughly breaks even
  where it normally loses 4.6%. There's no directional edge either way.
- **Budget:** a big intraday move (1.29% vs 0.73% normal), usually DOWN on the day: the put made +22%, and the straddle won 4 of 10
  times (avg +4.5%). n=10 is small.
- **Ordinary nights:** every option buy loses after decay and costs, except the call held to the next open (flat:
  India's overnight drift is upward).

## 3. Delta-hedged straddle, Nifty, every trading day 2020-26 (1-min re-hedge, band 0.10)

Buy the ATM weekly straddle at 15:25, short delta in futures, hold the overnight gap, re-hedge on 1-min bars, exit
at the next day's 15:25. The short book is the mirror.

| Group (n) | Implied move | Realised move | Long gross | Costs | Long net | Short net |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ordinary (1,571) | 1.08% | 0.84% | -1.0% | 6.3% | -7.2% | -5.3% |
| RBI (42) | 1.12% | 0.90% | -3.3% | 3.2% | -6.5% | +0.1% |
| FOMC (43) | 0.98% | 0.87% | 0.0% | 3.1% | -3.1% | -3.1% |
| Budget (8) | 1.13% | 1.50% | +27.2% | 11.4% | **+15.8%** | -38.6% |
| Election (1) / exit poll (1) | | | +158% / +30% | | +153% / +27% | |

By days to expiry: 1 day left (Mondays): costs eat 18% of the tiny premium (long -23.8%, short -12.2%); 4-7 days
left: long -2.7%, short -3.6%.

What this says:
- **Options on Nifty are priced rich.** VIX implied 1.08% a day versus 0.84% realised, and that held in every year
  2020-2026 (a volatility risk premium of about 22%).
- **Selling the hedged straddle still loses on a one-day hold.** Rare gap days pay the long side enormously and
  cannot be hedged overnight (2026-02-03 gap +4.8%: short -377% of premium; 2025-04-07 gap -5.0%: -259%; 2020-03-13
  -193%). Re-hedging 13 times a day costs another ~3-4% of premium.
- **Buying the hedged straddle works only on big scheduled shocks:** Budget (+15.8% avg, won 5 of 8, n=8),
  election results and exit polls. RBI and FOMC are priced correctly or too high.
- **Practical rules for V9:**
  1. Never trade the 1-day-to-expiry weekly; use the next weekly.
  2. Re-hedge on a wider band (0.20) or at fixed times to cut costs. This is a new pre-registered trial.
  3. Test SELL-and-hold-to-expiry with a defined-risk wing (iron fly) instead of a one-day hold, so the tail gap is
     capped. The premium is there; the overnight gap is the problem.
  4. Event long-vol only for Budget / election / exit-poll type days, and record the REAL quotes to check the
     model's IV assumption before believing it.
